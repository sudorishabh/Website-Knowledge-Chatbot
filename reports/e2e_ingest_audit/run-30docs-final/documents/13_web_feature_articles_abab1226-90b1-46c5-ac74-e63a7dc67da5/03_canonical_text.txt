India should retain a clear distinction between the compliance and offset segments of its emerging carbon market, to drive industrial decarbonisation while directing credible carbon finance to agriculture, forestry and ecosystem restoration, writes Mr Sayanta Ghosh, Senior Associate Fellow, Land Resources Division, The Energy and Resources Institute (TERI).
