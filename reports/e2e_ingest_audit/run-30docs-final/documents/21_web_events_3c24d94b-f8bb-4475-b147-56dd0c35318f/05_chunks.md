Minister of State for External Affairs

Shri Pabitra Margherita is an Indian politician, cultural producer, and Member of Parliament representing Assam in the Rajya Sabha. He currently serves as the Union Minister of State for External Affairs and Minister of State for Textiles, portfolios he assumed in June 2024. He attended the Textile Institute in Guwahati.
