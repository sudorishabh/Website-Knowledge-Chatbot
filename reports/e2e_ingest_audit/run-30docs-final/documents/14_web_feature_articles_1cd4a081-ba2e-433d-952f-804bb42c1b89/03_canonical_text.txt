Gujarat needs a dedicated State Mangrove Policy to strengthen fragmented conservation efforts, ensure long-term restoration, and integrate scientific data with coastal development and climate resilience. write Dr Ronak Rohitkumar Gadhvi, Associate Fellow and Mr Kapil Kumar, Senior Associate Fellow, Land Resources Division, The Energy and Resources Institute (TERI).
